# Hackathon Pitch & Judge Showcase Skill

When invoked to prepare presentation materials or pitch strategy:

1. **3-Minute Pitch Structure**:
   - **0:00 - 0:30 (The Hook & Pain Point)**: Real-world problem, relatable metric, why current tools suck.
   - **0:30 - 1:00 (The Solution & Secret Sauce)**: High-level concept and architecture diagram.
   - **1:00 - 2:15 (The Live POC Demo)**: Walkthrough of the live product (using pre-seeded sample data).
   - **2:15 - 2:45 (Business / Social Impact & Roadmap)**: Scalability, next steps, target audience.
   - **2:45 - 3:00 (Team & Wrap-up)**: Call to action, GitHub link, live Vercel URL.

2. **Judge Anticipated Q&A**:
   - "How do you handle API hallucination or downtime?" -> Show fallback architecture and validation schema.
   - "How is this different from existing tools?" -> Focus on your unique agentic loop or real-time pipeline.
   - "How will you monetize or scale this?" -> Mention unit economics and serverless edge architecture.

3. **Slide Deck Integration**:
   - Format talking points matching slides in `presentation/index.html`.
