# Brainstorming & Ideation Pipeline Skill

When invoked to brainstorm or evaluate ideas for HackYeah 2026:

1. **Problem Clarification**:
   - What is the real-world friction point?
   - Who is the specific primary user/persona?
   - Why have existing solutions failed or why is AI uniquely positioned to solve this now?

2. **The "Magic Moment" Definition**:
   - What is the single 10-second interaction that will make judges say "Wow"?
   - Ensure the architecture and UI place this moment front-and-center.

3. **Hackathon Scoring Matrix (1-10 on each)**:
   - **Innovation**: Is it novel or an unexpected combination of technologies?
   - **Technical Depth**: Does it show real engineering (agent loops, APIs, DB, async tasks)?
   - **Practical Value**: Can this become a real product or open-source tool?
   - **Demo Polish**: Can it be demonstrated reliably in 2 minutes without glitches?

4. **Scoping Output**:
   - Produce a **Must-Have** list (the Minimal Viable Hack) and a **Drop-List** (features to explicitly ignore to guarantee on-time completion).
